# Open Scribe Terms

> **Draft — not adopted, not legal advice, and not effective terms.**

Open Scribe is intended to be open-source software distributed under the MIT License, with direct-download macOS artifacts and a public informational website. This draft does not create an agreement and does not describe a released product.

Before release, professionally reviewed terms should address:

- license and third-party components;
- user responsibility for recording consent and applicable law;
- prohibition on stealth or unlawful capture;
- no representation that an in-app reminder satisfies legal obligations;
- optional remote providers and their separate terms/retention;
- model output limitations and the evidence-versus-interpretation boundary;
- acceptable website/download use;
- warranty, liability, export, sanctions, and jurisdiction language appropriate to the actual publisher;
- security disclosure, support, updates, termination, and contact details.

Do not insert a specific jurisdiction, publisher identity, legal address, or effective date until the responsible legal entity and review path are confirmed.

The native app and website must consume this canonical source or a deterministic projection; no separately edited public copy is allowed.
