# Open Scribe Design Contract

**Status:** research gate not executed; no visual direction selected.

The founding character is Mac-native, quiet, instrument-like, precise, typographic, spatial, local, trustworthy, and technically credible. Recording truth must be unmistakable without depending only on color. Review should read as a conversation document, not a dashboard. Attention selection should use bounded perimeter luminance and depth, not permanent crop-box framing.

## Required research gate

Before visual implementation:

1. inspect current Apple design references plus at least 12 relevant examples across Refero, Landbook, SiteInspire, Godly, and Mobbin;
2. record the exact mechanism learned from each example rather than copying a style;
3. propose three materially different directions;
4. select one with explicit rationale and accessibility implications;
5. define typography, spacing, color, state, motion, overlay, and responsive rules;
6. visually verify native and web surfaces at relevant sizes.

That work has not been performed in this scaffold, so starter colors, gradients, glass, cards, type, icons, and layout may not be treated as approved.

## Durable prohibitions

Avoid generic AI gradients, orb heroes, endless rounded cards, fake dashboards, card-per-utterance transcripts, pill overload, stock AI art, fake customers or metrics, decorative glass/grid/particles, and pixel-identical native/web rendering.

## Platform distinction

The macOS app should use native semantic structures and behavior. The website may explain the product with a separate visual composition. They share terminology, evidence semantics, and legal truth—not presentation code.
