## Diagnosis

There is already enough product framing to identify the next useful question.
The persona, hypothesis, and prototype plan describe one coherent review job,
an explicit target, a proposed intervention, and ways to test it. The founding
contract gives meaningful constraints, and the recorded recovery tie preserves
counterevidence. The opportunity cost of a Foundation Sprint now is spending
two days restating that framing instead of learning what happens in a real
review workflow. A strategic workshop may become useful later if evidence
exposes competing audiences or substantially different product directions.

The central shortfall is criteria 3 and 6. The available sources do not show
someone carrying out the job with their current tools, where effort or errors
arise, or whether the proposed flow addresses the important difficulty. The
persona's Low confidence and the unstarted human trial make this uncertainty
explicit. Technical preservation and synthetic recognition results establish
their stated component behavior; neither fills the customer-knowledge gap.
Historical interface references are useful but also do not supply that baseline.

Criteria 4 and 5 are unresolved logistics, not demonstrated personal failures.
The product owner can make decisions, but no evidence confirms two free days.
The prototype roles are placeholders for actual staffing, not three people
already available to attend a Foundation Sprint. A self-assessed knowledge
score is also absent; assigning one would manufacture an input. Confirm these
facts only if the evidence indicates that a two-day strategic workshop remains
the right intervention.

The smallest useful next step is one observed instance of the review job in
the operator's existing method, using deliberately selected material and a
content-free observation record. Capture the task, completed steps, assistance,
repeated work, and whether the result can be checked. That first case can expose
the next uncertainty, but it is not population validation or an automatic Go.
It also need not wait for a prototype build or Open Scribe installation. Keep
the distinction between current-method discovery, prototype comprehension,
and the qualified native comparison; none substitutes for the others.

## Recommended Preconditions (Conditional Go or Wait only)

These are recommendations for a later execution request. Owners are accountable
roles, not newly assigned people; dates are proposed checkpoints and create no
calendar event, outreach, or research session.

1. **Establish a concrete customer-workflow evidence base**
   (owner: product owner, with a research owner still to be named; target:
   **2026-10-28**). First attach any existing observed cases or research that
   answer the review-job question. If none exists, begin with one deliberately
   arranged walkthrough of the operator's current method. Done means the
   packet identifies the person's real task, actual tools, an observed
   bottleneck or a successful alternative, and the limits of the observation.
   Preserve a finding that the existing method is sufficient. One case is an
   entry point; gather further cases only where the strategic choice still
   lacks support or material counterexamples remain unresolved.

2. **Make the decision inputs usable**
   (owner: the named research owner or PM, accountable to the product owner;
   target: **2026-10-28**, after the first evidence pass). Link the observed
   cases and the relevant current alternative to the existing H-001 evidence
   record. Separate measured behavior, participant report, product claims,
   and assumptions. Obtain the product owner's own 1-10 knowledge assessment
   with its basis; a high score alone cannot replace sources. Avoid a new
   generic competitor inventory or another persona generated from the same
   assumptions. This closes criterion 6 only when the packet can inform the
   actual choice.

3. **Confirm that a Foundation Sprint would resolve a remaining strategic choice**
   (owner: product owner; target: **2026-10-28**). Name the competing bets and
   the commitment that the two days would enable. If the next question is
   still whether people can complete and prefer the already-framed review
   flow, continue toward the existing observation or trial path instead.
   Consider a Foundation Sprint when evidence warrants choosing between
   materially different audiences, problems, or approaches. Do not make
   completion of the full five-operator trial a universal prerequisite for
   strategy work; use the evidence needed for the actual choice.

4. **Confirm the human decision team only if the workshop is still justified**
   (owner: product owner, supported by a facilitator still to be named;
   target: **2026-10-28 readiness review**, then before any Day 1 booking).
   Done means a named Decider with authority and confirmed full-day
   availability for both days, plus a named and deduplicated 3-5-person core
   roster covering facilitation, product/domain knowledge, and customer
   evidence. Resolve role overlaps and actual date/location constraints.
   Unfilled prototype seats and AI agents do not establish these confirmations.

**Reassessment condition:** reopen this diagnostic when the critical knowledge
and input gaps are closed, a consequential strategic choice remains, and the
human participation facts are known. Go requires all eight criteria to be
supported; Conditional Go is limited to one or two specific, genuinely small
prep gaps. Calendar passage to October 28 alone does not change the verdict.
