## Product and architecture guards

- Implement one milestone at a time. Bootstrap may not silently expand into capture or ML.
- Reliability and recoverability precede intelligence.
- Swift owns Apple-only UI/platform adapters. Rust owns durable state, evidence, recovery, provider policy, and exports. UniFFI stays coarse; frame-rate media and pointer samples do not cross ordinary callbacks.
- Shared Rust crates must compile for `wasm32-unknown-unknown` and may not depend on native crates.
- Remote-provider selection is not content-scope authorization.
- No claim of local-only, recovery, grounding, deployment, signing, or release without the exact corresponding proof plane.
- Never introduce Python, FastAPI, React, Tauri, Electron, a localhost application server, implicit cloud processing, or continuous default screenshot retention.
