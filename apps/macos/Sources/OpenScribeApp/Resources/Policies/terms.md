## Scope and publisher

MLNavigator is the intended publisher of Open Scribe and its informational
website at open-scribe.app. Open Scribe is intended for deliberate capture,
preserved audio, local transcription, human review, evidence-linked playback,
and export. Source implementation, development qualification, supported
platforms, and an actual public release are different states. The current
capability manifest and exact release evidence govern availability.

## Recording and content responsibility

Use Open Scribe only where you have the permissions and authority required for
the recording, screen observation, import, processing, and sharing involved.
Requirements can differ with location, participants, workplace rules, content,
and use. Obtain the necessary participant consent and observe applicable
privacy, confidentiality, intellectual-property, and recording obligations.

An operating-system permission, in-app indicator, consent reminder, or deliberate
Record action does not establish that a particular recording is lawful. Do not
use the application for covert or unlawful capture, unauthorized surveillance,
credential theft, or access to content you are not entitled to process.

## Review, accuracy, and preservation

Recognition, speaker attribution, screen-text reduction, summaries, and other
model output can be wrong or incomplete. Human corrections are distinct from
original hypotheses. Derived interpretations are distinct from source evidence;
an evidence link helps review but does not establish that an interpretation is
true. Review the underlying media and applicable context before relying on an
output or representing it as an authoritative record.

Pre-release software can fail. Keep appropriate independent copies of important
originals, and verify exports and restoration before relying on them. Deletion
may move audio to Trash; it does not erase imported originals, exports,
backups, or provider records. Portable export support is limited to the formats
and preservation guarantees actually documented for that release.

## Optional providers and external services

No account or remote AI provider is required by the core local workflow.
Enabling a provider is optional and does not by itself authorize every category
of content. Review the actual sharing scope, provider terms, fees, retention,
training practices, and applicable model license before sending content.

GitHub downloads, model hosts, Apple services, and any future update or provider
service are operated under their own terms. This draft does not promise their
availability, pricing, deletion behavior, or response time. Open Scribe's
privacy notice describes the relevant local and optional network paths and
their current qualification limits.

## Website, security, and support

Use the website and download infrastructure without unauthorized intrusion,
malware distribution, or interference with others. Reuse of Open Scribe source
remains governed by its license. Cite research or other website material under
the license or permission that accompanies that material.

Report suspected security defects through the private path described in
SECURITY.md. Do not put private conversations, credentials, or sensitive
reproductions in a public issue. GitHub private reporting is enabled; intake
handling, response ownership, and security-policy adoption still require review.

No service-level agreement, supported release, security-response deadline, or
update-delivery commitment has been adopted. Updates must follow the signed,
verified release process; a source change is not an installed update.
